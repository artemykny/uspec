# Glossary

## Terms

| Term | Meaning | Not to confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| Basket | Items a customer has picked from one restaurant before paying. | Order |
| Order | A paid request for food from one restaurant, delivered to one address. | Basket |
| Payout | Money Forkly transfers to a restaurant for its delivered orders, minus commission. | Refund |
| Refund | Money returned to a customer for an order or part of it. | Payout |
| Courier shift | A period in which a courier is available to accept jobs. | — |
